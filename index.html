Key Features

Everything you need to manage venues

From browsing available venues to getting your booking approved — vAccess handles it all.

🗓️

Easy Booking

Select a venue, choose a date and time slot, fill in your details and submit — all in a guided multi-step flow.

📍

Venue Availability

See a visual calendar with available and booked time slots. No guesswork — know instantly what's free.

✅

Admin Approval

Admins can review, approve, or reject bookings with remarks — keeping the process transparent and accountable.

🔔

Real-time Notifications

Get notified instantly when your booking status changes or when admin adds feedback to your request.

📋

Booking History

All your bookings — past and present — are tracked in one place with full details and approval history.

🔐

Role-Based Access

Students and faculty book venues; admins manage and approve. Each role gets a tailored, focused interface.

How It Works

Four simple steps

Booking a college venue has never been this straightforward.

1
🎓

Register & Log In

Create your account as a student or faculty member. Admins receive separate credentials.

2
🏛️

Browse Venues

Filter venues by type, capacity, category and availability. Pick the one that suits your event.

3
📅

Select Date & Time Slot

Use the interactive calendar and time-slot grid to pick an available slot for your event date.

4
🔔

Track Approval

Submit your booking and track its status in real-time. Get notified when the admin approves or rejects it.

Venues

Wide range of campus venues

From intimate conference rooms to large auditoriums — find the right space for every occasion.

🎭

Auditoriums

Large event spaces for cultural programs, convocations, and guest lectures. Capacity: 200–500.

🎓

Seminar Halls

Academic halls equipped with AV systems for workshops, seminars, and presentations. Capacity: 80–120.

💼

Conference Rooms

Compact meeting rooms for committee discussions, project reviews, and faculty meetings. Capacity: 20–30.

⚽

Sports Grounds

Football fields, basketball courts and open grounds for sports events and team activities.

🔬

Laboratories

Computer labs and electronics labs available for technical workshops and training sessions.

🌳

Open Grounds

Large outdoor areas for fests, cultural events, and gatherings. Capacity: up to 2000 people.
